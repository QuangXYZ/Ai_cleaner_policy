👋 Introduction & Overview

At KasumiLabs, we believe file cleaners and phone maintenance tools should protect your personal autonomy. This Privacy Policy explains how Phone Cleaner AI (the "Application") handles your information.

IF YOU DO NOT AGREE WITH THE TERMS OF THIS PRIVACY POLICY, PLEASE DO NOT ACCESS OR USE THE APPLICATION.

Phone Cleaner AI was engineered with a strict 100% on-device AI architecture. All scanning algorithms, photo comparison models, video compression, and contact duplicate searches execute locally on your physical device. We never transmit your private photos, videos, contacts, or documents to remote servers.

🔒 What We DO NOT Collect

Phone Cleaner AI does NOT collect, harvest, view, upload, or sell your personal files or media content.

Specifically, the following data never leaves your device:

  • Personal Photos & Videos — Camera roll photos, screenshots, albums, and video recordings are analyzed locally in volatile memory and never transmitted over the internet.
  • File Contents — Downloads, APKs, cache logs, archives, audio files, and documents stay strictly in your device storage.
  • Contacts & Address Book — Contact names, phone numbers, and notes never leave your handset.
  • Biometric Data & Passwords — We never access fingerprint, face authentication, or personal account passwords.
  • Precise GPS Location — We do not track or record your geographic coordinates.
🛡️ Key Guarantee: Your files remain exclusively yours. All duplicate detection and compression operations happen on your phone's processor. No user photos or private files are ever uploaded to cloud servers.

🧠 On-Device AI Architecture

Phone Cleaner AI uses local machine learning and perceptual algorithms designed to run efficiently on mobile processors:

  • Duplicate & Similar Photo Clustering — Our engine computes perceptual mathematical hashes (pHash/dHash) locally in RAM. Duplicate images are grouped side-by-side without transmitting photos to any cloud service.
  • Hardware Video Compressor — Utilizes your phone's native Android MediaCodec hardware encoder to re-compress bloated videos locally without quality loss.
  • Speaker Dust & Water Ejector — Emits specialized low-frequency acoustic soundwaves and sinusoidal pulses to dislodge moisture droplets and dust particles from physical speaker grills.
  • Contacts De-duplication & Local Backup — Merges duplicate phone numbers and exports standard VCF backup files stored securely in your local documents directory.

🗑️ Safe File Deletion & 30-Day Recovery Bin

We enforce strict safety rails to eliminate accidental file loss:

  • No Autonomous Deletions — Phone Cleaner AI never deletes any photo, video, contact, or file without your explicit preview and confirmation.
  • 30-Day Safety Recovery Bin — Cleaned items are held in the app's internal Safety Bin. You can review and restore any file within 30 days before automatic expiration.
  • Permanent Purge — When you manually empty the Trash Bin, the system invokes standard OS deletion to safely reclaim free storage space.
⚠️ User Responsibility: You retain full responsibility for verifying files before authorizing permanent deletion. We recommend keeping backups of irreplaceable media.
