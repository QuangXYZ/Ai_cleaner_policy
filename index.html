4 On-Device AI Architecture & Utilities

Phone Cleaner AI utilizes intelligent algorithms to help you keep your phone tidy, organized, and running smoothly. All intelligent modules run directly inside your mobile CPU/GPU:

A. Duplicate & Similar Photo Clustering

Our image clustering engine computes mathematical perceptual fingerprints (pHash/dHash) locally in volatile memory. Images with identical hashes are grouped as duplicates; images taken in burst mode with high visual similarity are categorized for easy side-by-side review. No photos are uploaded to any external server or AI training pipeline.

B. Smart Video Compression

The video compressor leverages your device's built-in Android hardware MediaCodec API to re-encode oversized video recordings into efficient formats. The compression process is executed completely on-device, and the original file is preserved until you choose to replace it.

C. Speaker Dust & Water Ejector

The Speaker Cleaner feature uses low-frequency acoustic soundwaves and sinusoidal sound pulses to physically dislodge trapped moisture, droplets, and dust particles from the device's acoustic speaker grills. This process is purely mechanical/acoustic and collects zero personal data.

D. Contacts Cleaner & Local VCF Backup

The Contacts module identifies duplicate contact entries, merges duplicate phone records upon user consent, and allows exporting backup contacts in standard VCF format stored locally in your chosen storage directory.

5 Safe File Deletion & 30-Day Recovery Bin

We prioritize file safety above all else. Unlike primitive cleaners that silently delete user data, Phone Cleaner AI enforces strict safety rails:

  • Zero Autonomous Deletions: The Application never removes any media, document, contact, or file without your explicit preview and confirmation.
  • Safe Recovery Bin: Files selected for cleanup can be placed into the app's internal Safety Bin. You can review and restore any mistakenly trashed file within 30 days before automatic expiration.
  • Permanent Purge: When you manually empty the Trash Bin, the system invokes standard OS unlinking to safely recover free storage space.
